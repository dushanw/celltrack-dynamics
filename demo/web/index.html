How to read it

  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, R resets the view.
  • space plays, ←/→ step one frame (2.5 min).
  • A trail that changes colour is an identity switch; a trail that stops is a cell that was lost, merged, left the field or divided.
  • Green ring = confirmed division (two daughter tracks start); magenta ring = death.
  • Hover a cell for its track id, state, speed and link confidence; click to highlight its whole lineage.
